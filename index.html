Part 3 · Full Campaign Story

A Different Way to Experience Winter

Winter changes how we move through the world. We layer up, carry extra clothing and constantly adjust to changing temperatures and conditions. Traditional winter clothing can be bulky, restrictive and inconvenient.

We believe there is room for a different approach: clothing that combines the comfort and protection we need with thoughtful technology that works naturally in the background.

That idea became Revival.

Part 3 · Full Campaign Story

Rethinking Winter Clothing

Revival began with a simple question: what if winter clothing could do more without becoming more complicated?

Instead of another conventional jacket, we imagined a flowing winter cape designed around freedom of movement, comfort and warmth. A garment that feels soft and natural to wear, while integrating technology directly into its design.

Our goal is to bring together warmth, weather protection, intelligent heating, rechargeable power and audio in one considered piece of clothing.

Part 3 · Full Campaign Story

Introducing Revival

Revival is a lightweight, knee-length winter cape with a naturally flowing silhouette and an integrated hood.

Its design is intentionally simple: a soft, warm interior, a technical outer shell and a single discreet clasp at the neck. There are no conventional arm openings. Your arms remain comfortably beneath the cape and extend out from the lower sides when needed.

The cape is designed to drape naturally rather than feel stiff or overly structured. Its integrated hood is adjustable, with the adjustment concealed within the design.

Part 3 · Full Campaign Story

Intelligent Warmth That Adapts

Revival is being designed around integrated heating zones distributed throughout the garment.

Planned heating areas include the chest, upper back and shoulders, lower back, sides and the back of the hood. The hood’s side areas are reserved for its integrated audio components.

The intended system will monitor the garment’s internal microclimate and surrounding conditions to help manage warmth. Independent heating zones are designed to adjust automatically, with manual controls available whenever you want to take over.

You will be able to select Low, Comfortable or High warmth settings. Manual selections are intended to remain in place until you return to Automatic mode. Safety limits will remain active regardless of the selected mode.

Part 3 · Full Campaign Story

Power Designed Around You

Revival is designed with two removable rechargeable battery packs housed in discreet, symmetrical pockets inside the chest area.

The two packs are intended to work together as one combined power supply for the garment’s integrated systems. They can be removed for charging and are designed to remain concealed while wearing the cape.

A power-saving mode is also planned to help manage energy when the battery level becomes low.

Battery capacity, operating time, charging performance and final electrical safety specifications will be established through engineering and testing.

Part 3 · Full Campaign Story

Your Soundtrack to Winter

Revival is also being designed to include two compact speakers integrated into the hood near the ears.

The intention is to make listening to music, podcasts and other audio more convenient without needing to wear separate headphones. The speakers will connect through Bluetooth.

Their final placement, sound quality, comfort, moisture protection and performance will be refined during development.

Part 3 · Full Campaign Story

Connected Comfort. Simple Control.

Revival is being designed to work as a connected garment, with an accompanying mobile app planned for more detailed control.

The intended app experience includes heating-zone adjustments, automatic and manual modes, battery monitoring, power-saving controls and audio settings.

The garment will also have physical controls for essential functions, including power and warmth adjustment. Basic garment operation is intended to remain available without a phone or internet connection.

Part 3 · Full Campaign Story

Built for Winter’s Elements

The outer shell is being developed with protection against wind, rain and wet snow in mind. The interior is intended to feel soft, warm and comfortable throughout both the cape and hood.

The overall garment is being designed to remain lightweight, with a natural flowing drape rather than the rigid feel of conventional technical outerwear.

Material selection, weather resistance, insulation, heating performance and durability will be refined through prototype development and testing.

Buy Better. Keep Longer. Replace Less.

We want Revival to be a product people can keep and use for years.

That means considering durability, repairability and the ability to replace or service electronic components and batteries where practical. Our long-term ambition is to create a garment that can be maintained rather than discarded when one component reaches the end of its useful life.

Part 8 · Risks and Challenges

Risks and Challenges

Revival combines garment construction with integrated heating, rechargeable power, electronic controls and Bluetooth audio. Bringing these elements together in a comfortable, reliable and durable garment will require engineering, prototyping and testing.

The final materials, heating performance, battery capacity, operating time, audio performance, weather resistance and manufacturing specifications have not yet been established. These details will be refined as development progresses.

Manufacturing timelines and costs may change as suppliers are selected and prototypes are evaluated. Component availability, testing requirements and production lead times may also affect the schedule.

We will approach development in stages, prioritize the core garment and heating system, and keep backers informed through regular project updates. If a significant change affects the product or estimated delivery, we will explain the situation and our plan.

Part 10 · FAQ Answers

FAQ (1 of 2)

What is Revival?

Revival is a technology-integrated winter cape designed to combine warmth, weather protection, rechargeable power, heating controls and Bluetooth audio in one flowing garment.

How does the heating work?

Revival is being designed with multiple integrated heating zones and automatic temperature management, with manual warmth settings available. The final heating specifications and performance will be established through development and testing.

How are the batteries powered?

The design uses two removable rechargeable battery packs housed in concealed chest pockets. They are intended to work together as one combined power supply. Final capacity and runtime will be confirmed during engineering.

Does Revival require a phone?

No. Basic garment operation is intended to work through physical controls without a phone or internet connection. A companion app is planned for more advanced settings.
